Blitz Rules

You and the AI race through the exact same shuffled sequence of questions, each at your own pace. Answer as many as you can before time runs out.

Every question is a single click: right or wrong, picking an option immediately moves you to the next question. There's no going back.

A correct answer is +1, a wrong answer is -1 - your score can go negative.

The AI "thinks" for a random amount of time on each question (faster and sharper on HARD) before locking in its own answer, right or wrong. Watch its live score in the side panel.

Whoever has the higher net score when the clock hits 0 wins the Blitz. A tie is a draw.

Pausing freezes the clock and the AI. Resume whenever you're ready, no limit on how many times.
