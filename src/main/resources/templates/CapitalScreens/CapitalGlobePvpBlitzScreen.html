Blitz vs Friend Rules

You and your friend race through the exact same shuffled sequence of capitals, independently and at your own pace, not turn by turn.

Click a country to select it, then press Enter or Confirm Guess to submit it. Unlimited guesses per capital, colored by proximity. Once you find it, you move straight to your next one. Press Skip to give up on one and move on.

A correct answer is +1, a skip is -1 - your score can go negative. No hints in this format.

You can't see your opponent's actual guesses, only their live net score and how many they've answered.

Whoever has the higher net score when the clock hits 0 wins the Blitz. A tie is a draw.

Either of you can hit PAUSE to freeze the match for up to 60 seconds. Each player gets one pause for the whole match, and it auto-resumes if nobody hits resume first.
