1v1 vs Friend Rules

You and your friend both try to find the exact same country, every round. Click it on the globe (highlighted in purple) then press Enter or Confirm Guess - whoever's guess is confirmed correct FIRST wins the round.

You can't see your opponent's actual guesses, only their live net score and attempt count.

First to reach 2 net correct wins the match.

No limit on guesses per round. Keep trying until you solve it, your opponent beats you to it, or you press Give Up Round.

When a round ends, both of you get a short window to hit READY - the next round starts once you're both ready, or after a few seconds either way.

Either of you can hit PAUSE to freeze the match for up to 60 seconds. Each player gets one pause for the whole match, and it auto-resumes if nobody hits resume first.
