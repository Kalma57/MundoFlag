Capital Locations Rules

You're shown a capital city. Can you guess which country it belongs to?

Click a country on the globe to select it (highlighted in purple), then press Enter or Confirm Guess to submit it. After each guess, the country will be colored based on how close it is to the target country.

You also get the distance in km between your guess and the target country.

If your guess shares a border with the target, you'll get a special notification.

Press Give Up to reveal the answer.
