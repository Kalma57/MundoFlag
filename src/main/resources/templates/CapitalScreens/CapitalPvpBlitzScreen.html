Blitz vs Friend Rules

You and your friend race through the exact same shuffled sequence of questions, independently and at your own pace, not turn by turn.

Click an answer - right or wrong, you move straight to the next question immediately, no retries.

A correct answer is +1, a wrong one is -1, your score can go negative.

You can't see your opponent's actual answers, only their live net score and how many they've answered.

Whoever has the higher net score when the clock hits 0 wins the Blitz. A tie is a draw.

Either of you can hit PAUSE to freeze the match for up to 60 seconds - each player gets one pause for the whole match, and it auto-resumes if nobody hits resume first.
