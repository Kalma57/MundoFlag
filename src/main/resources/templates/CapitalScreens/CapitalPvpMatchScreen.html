1v1 vs Friend Rules

Every round, you and your friend both answer the SAME question independently - it's not a race, both of you always get a shot. Your pick stays hidden until you've both answered.

A correct answer is +1, a wrong answer is -1, your score can go negative.

First to reach 2 net correct wins the match.

If your friend takes too long to answer, the round resolves anyway after a short window, treating their silence as a wrong answer - so nobody can stall the match forever.

Either of you can hit PAUSE to freeze the match for up to 60 seconds - each player gets one pause for the whole match, and it auto-resumes if nobody hits resume first.
