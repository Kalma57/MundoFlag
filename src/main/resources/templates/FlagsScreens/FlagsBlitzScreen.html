1 Minute Blitz Rules

You and the AI opponent both race through the exact same shuffled sequence of flags - but each at your own pace. There are no rounds: as soon as you guess a flag correctly (or skip it), you're straight on to the next one.

You can't see the opponent's actual guesses - only a live progress percentage on whatever flag it's currently working on, plus how many flags it has completed so far.

60 seconds on the clock. Whoever has correctly guessed the most flags when time runs out wins the Blitz - a tie is a draw.

You can press SKIP FLAG to give up on a flag you're stuck on and move straight to the next one - nobody gets credit for a skipped flag.
