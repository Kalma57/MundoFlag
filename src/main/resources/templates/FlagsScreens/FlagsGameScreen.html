Flags Rules

Can you identify the country by its flag?

Enter a country name and press GUESS. After each attempt you will see feedback comparing your guess to the correct flag.

🟢 Easy mode: the correct flag starts out black. Every area you match gets permanently revealed in its true color, building up across all your guesses - use those revealed areas as hints to narrow down your next guess. 🔴 Hard mode: areas that don't match are shown in black - only matches are revealed, and nothing carries over between guesses, so use each guess's matches as your only hint before it resets.

Guess correctly to win or press GIVE UP to reveal the answer.
