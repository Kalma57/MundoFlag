1v1 vs Computer Rules

You and the AI opponent both try to guess the exact same flag, every round. Whoever guesses it correctly first wins the round.

You can't see the opponent's actual guesses - only a live progress percentage and attempt count, so you know roughly how close they are without ever seeing their flags.

Best of 3: first side to win 2 rounds wins the match.

A running clock shows the total time elapsed in the match, just for reference - there's no time limit on this format.

You can press GIVE UP ROUND to concede a round you're stuck on - the opponent is credited with the win and you move straight to the next round.
