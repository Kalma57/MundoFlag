1 Minute Blitz Rules

You and the AI opponent both race through the exact same shuffled sequence of countries - but each at your own pace. There are no rounds: as soon as you guess a country correctly (or skip it), you're straight on to the next one.

You can't see the opponent's actual guesses - only a live proximity badge on whatever country it's currently working on, plus how many countries it has completed so far.

60 seconds on the clock. Whoever has correctly guessed the most countries when time runs out wins the Blitz - a tie is a draw.

You can press SKIP COUNTRY to give up on a country you're stuck on and move straight to the next one - nobody gets credit for a skipped country.
