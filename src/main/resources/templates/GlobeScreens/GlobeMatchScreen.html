1v1 vs Computer Rules

You and the AI opponent both try to guess the location of the exact same country, every round. Whoever guesses it correctly first wins the round.

You can't see the opponent's actual guesses - only a live proximity badge and attempt count, so you know roughly how close they are without ever seeing their guesses.

Best of 3: first side to win 2 rounds wins the match.

A running clock shows the total time elapsed in the match, just for reference - there's no time limit on this format.

You can press GIVE UP ROUND to concede a round you're stuck on - the opponent is credited with the win and you move straight to the next round.

There's no limit on how many guesses you get per round - keep trying until you solve it, run the clock out against the AI, or press GIVE UP.

💡 Stuck? HINT reveals one more letter of the country's name each time, at a random position.

A hint isn't entirely free, though: if you solve it after using hints, the round isn't over yet - the AI gets a grace period (20 seconds per hint you used) to still catch up and steal the round on its own clock. Solve it with no hints and you win immediately, no grace period at all.

When a round ends, the recap shows how long it took and the AI's final guess count - so you always know exactly what you were up against.
