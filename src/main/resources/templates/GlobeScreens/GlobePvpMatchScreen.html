1v1 vs Friend Rules

You and your friend both try to guess the exact same country, every round. Whoever guesses it correctly first wins the round.

You can't see your opponent's actual guesses - only their live proximity level and attempt count, so you know roughly how close they are without ever seeing their guesses.

Best of 3: first side to win 2 rounds wins the match.

A running clock shows the total time elapsed in the match, just for reference - there's no time limit on this format.

You can press GIVE UP ROUND to concede a round you're stuck on - your opponent is credited with the win and you both move straight to the next round.

There's no limit on how many guesses you each get per round - keep trying until you solve it, your opponent beats you to it, or you press GIVE UP.

💡 Stuck? HINT reveals one more letter of the country's name to you only, at a random position. Your opponent can see how many hints you've used, live, so it's not a secret.

A hint isn't entirely free, though: if you solve it after using MORE hints than your opponent, the round isn't over yet - they get a grace period (20 seconds per extra hint you used) to still catch up and steal the round. Any hint THEY take during that window costs them 20 seconds of their own remaining time. Use no more hints than your opponent and you win immediately, no grace period at all.

When a round ends, the recap shows how long it took and both sides' final guesses/hints - so you always get the full picture, win or lose.

Either of you can hit PAUSE to freeze the match for up to 60 seconds - each player gets one pause for the whole match, and it auto-resumes if nobody hits resume first.
