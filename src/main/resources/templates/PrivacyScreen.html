Privacy Policy

MundoFlag website and app · last updated November 2026

The short version

MundoFlag doesn't collect personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and nothing about you is sold or shared with anyone. We only keep anonymous counts (how many visits and games), explained below.

What the game stores, and why

  • A game session cookie Keeps your current game (your guesses, score and round) on the server while you play. It holds a random ID, not anything about you, and expires when you stop playing.
  • A "played today" cookie Remembers that you already took today's Daily Quiz. It only contains today's date and expires the next day.
  • Your first and last visit day, on your device Lets us count how many players come back. Once a day the game tells our server only "a visit happened", whether it was on the website or the app, and those two dates - no name, no ID, nothing that could tell you apart from anyone else.
  • Your Daily Quiz result, on your device So you can still share today's result later. It stays on your device and is replaced the next day.
  • Music settings on your device Whether the music is on, and where the song was, are saved in your browser / app only - they are never sent to us.

Anonymous counts

Our server keeps daily totals only: how many visits (website / app), how many of them were returning players, how many games of each kind were started, and how many Daily Quiz results were shared. These are plain numbers - they can't be linked to you or to your device.

Playing with a friend

A 1v1 room is identified by a short random code. Rooms hold only the game itself (flags, guesses, scores), not names or contact details, and are deleted from the server's memory after the match.

Technical information

Like any website, our server sees your device's IP address while it answers your requests. We don't store it in logs or use it to identify you. All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). The game's fonts, images and code are served by our own server - no third-party services are contacted while you play.

Children

MundoFlag is a geography game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information, it doesn't collect any from children either.
